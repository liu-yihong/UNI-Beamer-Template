# Logos and images

The UNI and Wilson College of Business logos are trademarks of the University of
Northern Iowa and are **not** included in this repository.

To show a logo on the title page:

1. Download the official lockup for your unit from the
   [UNI brand site](https://ur.uni.edu/uni-brand) (or request one from brand@uni.edu).
2. Save it here as `UNI CoB no-border.pdf`, or point `includes/tex/logo.tex` at your file name.

The template detects the file automatically; without it, the title page simply has no logo.
Logo files matching `UNI*.pdf` / `UNI*.png` in this folder are git-ignored so they are not
pushed by accident.

Put lecture images in subfolders here (e.g., `includes/pics/WK1/`).
